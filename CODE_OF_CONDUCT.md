# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project community regardless of
experience, identity, background, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and kind in technical discussion.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, authorship, and differing experience levels.
- Focus feedback on observable behavior and project outcomes.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and make room for others to participate.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Report concerns
to the repository owner through a private channel. The owner may remove content, restrict
participation, or take other proportionate action while protecting reporter confidentiality as far
as practical.

Project decisions may be appealed privately to the owner with relevant context. Retaliation against
a reporter or participant in an enforcement process is prohibited.
